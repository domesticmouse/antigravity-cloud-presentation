Big thank you to the WSTI Festi crew for having me today, and to everyone who came along to "Vibe Coding vs. Skill-Driven Engineering in Antigravity"! =)

Generating 2,000 lines of working code in 15 minutes is effortless now. But, the moment you close out that conversation and start a fresh session, every silent assumption that only lived in the chat transcript comes back to bite you.

For today's talk, I recorded two full runs building an app in Google Antigravity to track the bags of resistors, op-amps, and DIY synth kits cluttering up my workbench.

First up was a 30-minute, zero-ceremony vibe-coding spike. It looked great on the surface, right up until a fresh review session exposed all the domain edge cases it quietly guessed wrong. So, for run two, I rebuilt it across 10 focused sessions using Matt Pocock's agent skills (/wayfinder, /prototype, /to-tickets, worktree TDD, and 2-axis /code-review), before mapping the architecture onto Cloud Run and Firestore with Google's cloud skill set.

Turns out, you need both modes. Fast vibe coding is fantastic for figuring out what you actually want to build, and engineering hygiene is the sanity-saver once you want to keep it.
